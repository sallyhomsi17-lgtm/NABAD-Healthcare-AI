Hospital intake & operations • Lebanon-first

Better visibility before patients arrive.

NABAD is developing a messaging-first patient intake and hospital operations platform, designed around familiar conversations, structured information, and clinician-led care.

Explore the interactive demo ↗Discuss a pilot
The challenge

Patient uncertainty meets limited hospital visibility.

When patients and care teams communicate through fragmented channels, planning becomes harder. NABAD explores how structured pre-arrival information could support more organized hospital operations.

Patients

A familiar, accessible intake experience without requiring a new mobile app.

Clinical teams

Structured intake details for review, with clinicians retaining responsibility for decisions.

Operations

A clearer view of prospective patient demand and workflow status.

Designed workflow

From first message to coordinated review.

Current status

See the NABAD prototype in action.

Our current browser demo includes a simulated patient chat, rule-based urgency illustration, and a shared in-session hospital dashboard. It is a concept demonstration, not a live hospital system.

Safety and transparency: The prototype is not clinically validated, is not a medical device, and must not be used to diagnose, triage, or guide real patients. The dashboard's arrival times and surge predictions are illustrative.
